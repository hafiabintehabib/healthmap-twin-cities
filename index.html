Healthcare facilities of Islamabad & Rawalpindi

Study area

The study area covers the twin cities of Islamabad (Islamabad Capital Territory) and Rawalpindi (Punjab), Pakistan — roughly 33.45°–33.80° N and 72.80°–73.30° E. Together they hold over 5 million people and most of the region's major hospitals. This map retrieves health facilities live from OpenStreetMap so residents and planners can locate the nearest hospital, clinic, pharmacy, doctor, dentist or laboratory.
